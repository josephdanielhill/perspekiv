Why Perspektiv

AI doesn't design.
People do.

We use AI as our loom, our lathe, our brush – not as our voice. Every output is reviewed, refined, and signed off by a human who cares about the difference between good enough and intentional.

Surface-first, not template-first
We name the surface archetype before writing a single line – Monitor, Operate, Decide. The structure follows the interaction, not the other way around.
Anti-slop by design
We score every deliverable against a 10-point slop diagnostic. If it scores above 3, it doesn't ship. No gradients that pretend to be depth. No feature grids that pretend to be strategy.
Built for one person
This agency exists for exactly one client. Every pixel, every word, every decision serves that relationship.
