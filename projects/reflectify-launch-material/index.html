Two campaign launch assets for Reflectify – a private, offline-first personal reflection sanctuary that turns AI conversations into clear, visual growth summaries.

"Going Live" – Editorial Sanctuary Hero Card

The primary launch card focuses on an emotional, outcome-driven hook: turning chaotic AI conversations into a clear, honest picture of personal growth before entering performance reviews.

Features a prominent Reflectify brand mark and logotype directly above the headline, an unadorned website hero illustration frame (*Reflection Path*), and a clean trust badge highlighting 100% free, local-first browser privacy.

"A Tour of Reflectify" – 3-Step Guided Workflow Triptych

The exploration card guides users through Reflectify's three-step unhurried workflow:
- Step 1: Copy Prompt & Reflect – Pick a framework (Job or Personal Reflection) and run a guided reflection with any AI model (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or local LLMs).
- Step 2: Upload Reflection JSON – Drop or paste the structured JSON summary directly into Reflectify without logging in or sending data to servers.
- Step 3: Generate Summary – Instantly render a visual reflection card with energy level tracking, core values alignment, career timeline, and key quotes.

Process & Design Iterations

The design evolved through collaborative feedback rounds to ensure absolute alignment with Reflectify's brand identity:
- Organic Nature Backdrop: Expanded botanical artwork across the full canvas as a 28% opacity faded wallpaper.
- Noise Elimination: Stripped away top/bottom meta bars and interactive browser chrome to maintain an unhurried, spacious editorial feel.
- High-Legibility Feed Typography: Scaled triptych description copy to 32px for crisp mobile readability on social feeds.
- Soft, Human Tone: Softened corporate "interview" vocabulary to warm "guided reflection" language.
